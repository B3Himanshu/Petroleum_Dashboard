Dashboard Enhancement Changes – Reference for Other Dashboards
================================================================


1. Background & Visuals
   - Changed dashboard background from dark/black to white/light.
   - Set CSS root tokens to light mode (--background: light off-white).
   - Applied light gradient backdrop (soft blue/purple gradients).
   - Ensured all charts, tables, and KPI cards are readable on white background.


2. Volume & Value Breakdown (% of Total)
   - Added "% of total" column next to volume and value in fuel grade breakdown tables.
   - Volume table: Grade Name | Volume | % of total fuel volume.
   - Profit table: Grade Name | Value | % of total fuel sales.
   - Tooltip shows: "X% of total fuel volume" / "X% of total fuel sales".


3. PPL Wording Update
   - Renamed PPL KPI cards:
     * Card 1: "Gross PPL" (subtitle: "Per litre before O/H")
     * Card 2: "PPL after O/H" (subtitle: "Net per litre after O/H")
   - Updated chart legends to match: "Gross PPL" and "PPL after O/H".
   - Mobile label shortened to "After O/H" for space.


4. Gross Profit Display
   - Main KPI card shows only gross profit (no revenue/cost on the card face).
   - Added percentage alongside profit figure.
   - NOTE: Breakdown modal still needs work — currently shows Sales & Cost columns.
     Target: show only profit per category with % of total profit.


5. Fuel Grade Pie Charts (Side by Side)
   - Removed the navigation button that toggled between volume and profit pie charts.
   - Both pie charts now display side by side in a single card.
   - Layout: stacked on mobile (1 column), side by side on desktop (2 columns).
   - Left: Volume pie chart + table. Right: Profit pie chart + table.


6. PPL vs Actual Chart Title & Labels
   - Changed chart title from "PPL vs Actual PPL vending out OH" to use:
     * "Gross PPL" (was "PPL" / "Avg PPL")
     * "PPL after O/H" (was "Actual PPL")
   - Updated all legend labels and axis references to match.


7. Site Photos (Google Maps Integration)
   - Added Google Maps embed in site comparison cards.
   - Defined site coordinates (lat/lng) in a constants file.
   - Fallback chain: Local photo -> Google Maps embed -> MapPin icon placeholder.
   - NOTE: Currently shows map view, not Street View photos. Full photo gallery still pending.
