3. GROSS PPL AND PPL AFTER O/H
================================================================================
Card:
  Title:        "Gross PPL and PPL after O/H"
  Format:       Pence per litre (p) — 2 decimal places
  Main Values (stacked):
    Line 1 label: "Gross PPL"
    Line 1 value: 14.87p                    (green text)
    Line 2 label: "PPL after O/H"
    Line 2 value: 10.34p                    (emerald text, larger)
  Action:       "View breakdown ->"

  Fallback (no volume data): shows as % margin instead of pence.

Breakdown Modal: "PPL after O/H — Breakdown"
  SECTION 1: Overhead cost by category
    Columns: NAME | VALUE
    Rows:
      Row: "Wages"                           £XX,XXX
      Row: per overhead nominal code         £X,XXX
      ... (all overhead codes excl. 8200-8207 depreciation, 7750 loan)
      Row: "Total Overheads"                 £XXX,XXX     (bold total)

  SECTION 2: Calculation box (highlighted)
    "Fuel profit after overheads (£)"
    "{fuelProfit} − {totalOverheads} = {fuelProfitAfterOH}"

  SECTION 3: Summary
    Row: "Gross PPL"                         XX.XXp
    Row: "OH Deduction"                      -XX.XXp
    Row: "PPL after O/H"                     XX.XXp       (bold total)

  Formulas:
    Gross PPL = Fuel Profit / Fuel Volume * 100
    OH Deduction = |Overheads| / Fuel Volume * 100
    PPL after O/H = (Fuel Profit - |Overheads|) / Fuel Volume * 100

VALUE FORMATTING RULES
================================================================================
Currency (£):
  Show M mode:  £1.25M, £450K, £5.69M
  Exact mode:   £1,234,567.89 (commas, 2 decimals)

Volume (Litres):
  Show M mode:  4.70 ML, 279.03 KL, 21.29 KL
  Exact mode:   4,700,000.00 L (commas, 2 decimals)
  1 ML = 1,000,000 L
  1 KL = 1,000 L

PPL (Pence Per Litre):
  Always:       14.87p, 10.34p (2 decimals + "p")

Percentage (%):
  In cards:     0.8%, 96.8% (1 decimal)
  In modals:    89.5%, 0.3%, 10.2% (1 decimal)

================================================================================
BREAKDOWN MODAL COLUMN LAYOUT
================================================================================
All modals use a 3-column grid:
  grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)_auto_3.5rem]

  Column 1: NAME    — left-aligned, truncates with ellipsis if long
  Column 2: VALUE   — right-aligned, tabular-nums, font-medium
  Column 3: %       — right-aligned, tabular-nums, text-muted-foreground

  Normal row:  py-2 text-sm border-b border-border/60
  Total row:   font-semibold, border-primary/25, bg-primary/5, rounded-md
  Section:     text-[10px] font-semibold uppercase tracking-wide, pt-2 pb-1
  Sub-section: pl-2, border-l-2 border-emerald-500/35 (indented with green bar)
